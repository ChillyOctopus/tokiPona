toki pona dictionary

toki pona is a small language, first published in 2001, with only about 120 core words, so each one covers a lot of ground: pona is good, simple, fixing, and thanks. Here are all words the translator knows, with meanings drawn from lipu Linku, the community’s dictionary, and wasona, a free course.

How to read an entry
