The dictionary

How it works

  1. Words. The text is split into sentences, and each word is looked up in a 181-word dictionary. Type one word on its own to see all its meanings.
  2. Grammar. A grammar finds every reading of the sentence and ranks them. When there’s more than one, the arrows step through them.
  3. Checks. Each reading is checked against wasona’s rules, and anything off is explained.
  4. English. The reading is put into English, construct by construct.

The English is literal. toki pona doesn’t mark tense or number, so verbs come out in the present and nouns in the singular unless the sentence says otherwise, and a word’s most common meaning wins over the one its context might want. English to toki pona is still to come.

The parser, checker, and translator are Python, run in your browser by Pyodide. The code is on GitHub. Its sentences, rules, and dictionary draw on wasona and lipu Linku, under CC BY-SA 4.0.
